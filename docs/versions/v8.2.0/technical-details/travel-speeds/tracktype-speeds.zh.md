# Tracktype 速度
tracktype 对应 OSM [tracktype](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Key:tracktype) 标签的取值。
本表汇总了 [speed value files][svf] 中的取值。

_（所有取值单位均为 km/h）_

  | Tracktype \ 配置方案 -> | driving-car | driving-hgv | cycling-regular|
  |:----------------------:|:-----------:|:-----------:|:--------------:|
  |         grade1         |      40     |      40     |       18       |
  |         grade2         |      30     |      30     |       12       |
  |         grade3         |      20     |      20     |        8       |
  |         grade4         |      15     |      15     |        6       |
  |         grade5         |      10     |      10     |        4       |

[svf]: https://github.com/GIScience/openrouteservice/tree/main/ors-engine/src/main/resources/resources/services/routing/speed_limits
